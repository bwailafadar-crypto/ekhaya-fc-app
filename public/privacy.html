Ekhaya FC official app

Privacy Policy

Effective 16 October 2026. This policy explains what the Ekhaya FC app (the website ekhaya-fc-app.web.app and the Android app "Ekhaya FC", package mw.ekhayafc.app) collects, why, and the choices you have. The app is run by Ekhaya FC, Blantyre, Malawi.

What we collect

InformationWhy
Name, email address (from your sign-up form, or from your Google account if you choose "Continue with Google") and, if you give it, phone numberTo create your fan account, sign you in, show your name on fan features (predictions, Fan Family) and contact you about your account.
Profile photo (only if you add one)Shown on your Fan Profile.
Your activity in the app: score predictions, man-of-the-match votes, poll votes, reactions, stadium check-ins, players you follow, your district branch, who invited youTo run Fan Zone, leaderboards, fan points and Fan Family.
A notification token for your device and your alert choicesTo send match, news and goal alerts you switched on.
Error reports: the error message, the page, app version and browser/phone typeTo find and fix problems.
Membership details (when membership opens): tier, payment reference, amount, status, phone number used for mobile moneyTo take your membership payment and issue your member card. We never see or store your card number or mobile-money PIN.

While you are signed in, the app also records when you last used it and how many minutes you spend in it (only while it is on your screen). Only the club's superadmin can see this; it helps the club understand how fans use the app.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos library, microphone or files. The app has no advertising and we do not sell your information.

Who processes it for us

  • Google Firebase (hosting, sign-in, database and notifications). Data is stored on Google servers.
  • PayChangu, a Malawi payment company, when you pay for a membership with Airtel Money, TNM Mpamba, card or bank.
  • YouTube and Facebook only when you choose to play a club video or open the club's Facebook posts. Their own privacy policies apply.

Who can see it

Your email, phone and profile are visible only to you and the club's app administrators. Your name is shown to other fans next to your predictions, check-ins and Fan Family card. Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and access is limited by security rules on the database.

How long we keep it

We keep your account until you delete it. Error reports are deleted after 90 days. Paid membership and payment records are kept for the club's financial records as the law requires, even after you delete your account.

Deleting your account

In the app: More → Fan Profile → Delete my account. This deletes your sign-in, profile, fan card and alerts straight away, and your votes, predictions and check-ins within 7 days. See how to delete your account, including if you cannot sign in.

Children

The app is for football supporters aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • Change your name, phone or photo in Fan Profile.
  • Switch alerts off in Fan Profile or in your phone's settings.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct or delete it.

Contact

Message Ekhaya FC on its official Facebook page: facebook.com/EkhayaFC, or speak to the club office at Chiwembe Stadium, Blantyre.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
