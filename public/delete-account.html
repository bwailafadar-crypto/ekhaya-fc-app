Delete your account

You can delete your Ekhaya FC app account (Android app "Ekhaya FC" and ekhaya-fc-app.web.app) at any time.

In the app

  1. Open the app and sign in.
  2. Tap More → Fan Profile.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete my account.
  4. Enter your password, type DELETE and tap Delete for ever.

If you cannot sign in

Send a message to Ekhaya FC on its official Facebook page, facebook.com/EkhayaFC, saying "Delete my app account" with the email address you signed up with. We confirm and delete it within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Straight away: your sign-in, name, email, phone, profile photo, Fan Family card and alert settings.
  • Within 7 days: your predictions, votes, reactions, stadium check-ins, fan points and invites.

What is kept

Records of paid memberships and payments (amount, date, reference) are kept for the club's financial records as the law requires. Nothing else is kept.
